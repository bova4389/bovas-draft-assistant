Live sync pulls picks as they happen. It does not move the team columns — use Sync draft order above for that.
Each row's A (ADP) and S (Sleeper rank) numbers compare to our own rank: green means the room usually lets them fall past our rank (fine to wait); red means someone else will likely take them first.
split on a row is how far apart our three ranking sources are on that player — their highest rank minus their lowest. Split 2 means FantasyPros, Yahoo and JYJ all agree; split 60 means one source likes him five rounds more than another, so the blended rank is an average of two very different opinions rather than a settled one. It only appears at 25 or above, where the disagreement stops being noise. High-split players are where the sleeper and bust cases live.
